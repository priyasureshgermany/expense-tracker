What’s new

New category

Custom categories work everywhere the built-in ones do — entries, recurring rules, budgets and the charts. One that's already used by an entry can't be deleted until those entries are retagged.

Budgets

An amount here is what you allow yourself per period for that category. Every expense you log against it draws it down. Leave a category blank if you don't want to budget it — one-off bills like rent are better as recurring entries.
Any expense that isn't a recurring bill and has no budget of its own is drawn from this one — so Education, a card payment or a shop you've no category for all come out of it, while keeping their own category on the entry. Being the cushion, it's the one budget whose unspent part isn't counted as expected spending on the dashboard.
