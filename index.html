Scan receipt

Paste the receipt text and it will fill in the prices: each line is matched to an item on your list, and anything it can't match is added to the end of the list with its price.
Reading a photo of a receipt isn't supported: on-device text recognition would need an OCR library, which this app deliberately doesn't bundle so it stays a single offline file. Most shops e-mail a digital receipt you can copy the text from.
